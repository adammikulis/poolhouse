Live runs the decision model (or a plain-code controller) on a scenario now. Recorded replays a real model run from a file. Whatever is chosen here is what the room shows.

Dotted verticals mark scenario events; tick marks along each chart are decisions (amber abstained, red interlock). Thin lines are what the gauges read; thick lines are the plant's true state.

Each tile is a channel position tinted by the layout's flux weight times reactor power (power) or by hottest-channel temperature scaled by that weight (temperature). A rod's filled disc grows with insertion. White rings mark rods the model just moved; red crossed rings are stuck, amber rings are slow drives. Bubbles scale with void fraction.
