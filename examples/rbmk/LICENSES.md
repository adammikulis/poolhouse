The pinned version and the sha256 of the three vendored files are in `room/vendor/vendor.json` (they are byte-identical to
the npm package three@0.186.1) and the repository's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` lists them; `scripts/notices.py --check` fails on an
unlisted or changed file.

No third-party 3D model, texture, font or photograph is included. `room/room.glb.gz` is generated by
`generator/build_room.py` run in Blender 5.3 (the GLB records its exporter as `Khronos glTF Blender I/O v5.3.41` and a hash of the
generator sources) and is under the repository licence (Apache-2.0). The pictures in `viewer/stills/` (the 2D views and the id
masks) are rendered from that same generated room by `generator/render_views.py` in Blender 5.3, copy nothing from a photograph, and
are under the repository licence too.
