Published descriptions used for the content (read, not copied): the IAEA INSAG-7 report on the Chernobyl accident for the
roles of the control sections, the rod types and counts, the AZ-5 emergency protection, the operational reactivity margin and
its minimum of 15 rod equivalents, and the positive scram effect.

No existing 3D model of an RBMK or Chernobyl control room under CC0, CC BY, MIT or Apache-2.0 was found (Sketchfab, Poly
Pizza, Kenney, OpenGameArt, Blend Swap, Thingiverse, Wikimedia Commons, GitHub, itch.io). The control-room models that exist
are paid, editorial-only or carry no licence. The room is therefore built from scratch.
